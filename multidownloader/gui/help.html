Getting started

Three steps: paste, check Save to, Download.

  1. Paste links into the Links box. Any text that contains links works: a chat export, an e-mail, notes. Import file... reads a .txt, .csv, .json or web page.
  2. The line under the box says what was found, for example "12 links found: Instagram 5, X (Twitter) 4. 2 duplicates removed."
  3. Check Save to, then select Download. Links you saved before are marked "Already saved" and not downloaded again.

Supported: YouTube, X (Twitter), Instagram, Threads, TikTok, Facebook, Reddit, Google Drive and Bluesky.

Simple and full view

The simple view is one box and one button; the full view adds the queue, sign-ins and options.

Switch from the ? menu or with Ctrl+Shift+V. In the simple view, "switch to full view" is under the result line. The app opens in the view you used last.

The queue

One row per link, with its status in words and a symbol, never colour alone.

StatusMeaning
… WaitingNot started yet. Links from one platform wait their turn.
↓ 45 %Downloading.
✓ SavedSaved and listed in the run folder.
= Already savedSaved in an earlier run; not downloaded again.
✕ FailedNot saved. The Result column says why and what to do.
■ StoppedYou selected Stop before it started.
⊘ SkippedThe site is not supported yet.

Add notes (optional) above the queue saves what, where and who with the next run: event or topic, place, collected by (a name or pseudonym) and notes. Add note... in a link's menu adds a note to that link. Notes stay in the run folder. Leave out names if sharing them could put someone at risk.

Enter or a double-click opens the file. The context menu (right-click, Shift+F10 or the Menu key) has Open file, Open folder, Copy link, Add note, Make a playable copy, Copy error, Fix sign-in, Retry, Download again and Remove from list. Right-click a column heading and choose Fit columns to reset the column widths. Retry failed (F5) tries every failed link again.

Sign-ins

Public posts need no sign-in. When one is needed, the app offers to set it up after the run.

Most platforms' terms do not allow automated downloading. Each platform shows its terms notice once, and you confirm you will use this only where you have a right to. A sign-in stays on this computer, readable only by your Windows account; Remove deletes it.

  • Sign in here opens the platform's own sign-in page in a small window. Your password manager may not fill this window. Paste works.
  • Use my Firefox sign-in copies the sign-in from Firefox, where your password manager works.
  • Import a cookies file takes a cookies.txt exported from your browser. Only that platform's cookies are kept.

The sign-in window closes after 10 minutes. A minute before, the app asks "Still there?" and Keep waiting gives you 10 more minutes.

Options

Ctrl+, opens Options. Changes apply to the next download.

  • Video quality: the original, up to 1080p, up to 720p, or audio only. Photos are always the original.
  • What to save: post details (account, date, caption) next to the media, thumbnails, and the uploader's own subtitles. Keep files exactly as the platform served them (on by default) means no repairs or re-encoding; if such a file does not play, use Make a playable copy in its menu, which writes a second file and keeps the original.
  • Folders and file names: by platform and account, by platform, or all in one folder; optionally the post title in file names.
  • Speed: how many links at once (never two from the same platform), and a short pause between links from one platform.
  • Appearance: same as Windows, light or dark, from the next start. Windows High Contrast is always followed.

Reports and the run folder

Every run has its own folder with a list of what was saved and each file's fingerprint.

Verify files re-hashes every file of the last run and checks the custody log; it says "All 214 files match." or lists what changed. Export package... copies the run into a standard archive package (BagIt) for handing over, and checks it. The package includes the account names of the people in the posts, so share it only with people who need it.

The run folder (_runs in your download folder) has manifest.jsonl and manifest.csv (one line per link: where it came from, when, which files) and SHA256SUMS.txt. Export report writes one web page and the CSV. Account names of the people in the posts are left out of the report unless you choose to include them.

Updates

Old versions of yt-dlp are the most common reason downloads stop working.

Once a day the app asks pypi.org for the newest version numbers of yt-dlp and gallery-dl. Nothing about you or your links is sent. A bar appears when an update is needed; How to update shows what to do. Turn it off in Options.

When a link fails

The Result column says what happened in plain words. The most common ones:

MessageWhat to do
This post needs you to be signed inSelect Fix sign-in, or add one in Sign-ins.
did not accept your sign-inThe sign-in expired or was signed out. Sign in again.
is limiting requestsNothing: the app waits and tries again by itself. Fewer links at once helps.
YouTube is limiting this computerWait an hour or more, then try with one link at once.
This post was deleted or the link is wrongOpen the link in your browser to check it.
This post is privateSign in with an account that can see it.
blocked in your regionThe platform does not show it in your country.
Could not connectCheck your internet connection, then Retry.
Could not save the fileChoose another folder: the drive is full, read-only or the path is too long.
is not installedA component is missing (ffmpeg, Deno, gallery-dl). See the README.
needs an updateUpdate yt-dlp: see Updates above.

For a bug report, use ?, Copy diagnostics: versions and the last log lines, with sign-ins removed.

Privacy and safety

No telemetry. The app only talks to the platforms you download from and, once a day, pypi.org.

Manifests can contain the account names of people in the posts. Keep the download folder private, and turn on drive encryption (BitLocker on Windows, FileVault on macOS). Respect each platform's terms and your local law; this tool is meant for preserving public evidence and your own content.

Accessibility

Keyboard, contrast and scaling work today; screen readers do not fully read the window yet.

Every control works with the keyboard, focus is always visible, and text meets WCAG 2.2 AA contrast in the light, dark and High Contrast looks. The window scales at 150 % and 200 %. The window toolkit (Tk 8.6) does not announce status changes to screen readers, so the final result also goes into the window title. The command line (multidownloader-batch) works fully with screen readers.
